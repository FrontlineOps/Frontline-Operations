Frontline Operations Field Guide Campaign doctrine and sustainment

01 / Campaign Rhythm

One campaign, one main effort

The theater commander advances through a deliberate cycle instead of sending every force toward every fight. A main effort can be joined by up to two supporting operations when the theater can sustain them.

Lull

Reorganize, refill logistics, restore combat power, and select the next reachable objective.

Prepare

Reserve operation funds, stage forces, secure routes, and identify the intended objective or threat sector.

Assault

Commit each operation on its assigned axis while replacements draw from that operation's allocation.

Secure

Hold the captured area, defeat remaining resistance, and prevent an immediate reversal.

Consolidate

Integrate the objective and establish forward sustainment when the line has advanced beyond existing support.

Recovery

Release unused funds, rebuild depleted formations, and return the theater to LULL.

Only one operation is the main effort. Force readiness, connected logistics, treasury headroom, distinct axes, and defensive pressure determine whether support efforts open or withdraw. Connected player count is never part of that decision.

02 / Your Role

Choose work that changes the campaign

Players are the flexible force that connects command intent to battlefield results. You can reinforce the main effort, protect its support network, or develop a future opportunity.

Main Effort

Stage during PREPARE, attack during ASSAULT, and remain through SECURE. Leaving immediately after capture can allow the objective to collapse.

Supporting Effort

Attack a separate command-selected axis that widens the offensive without replacing the main effort.

Support Area

Protect source objectives, routes, depots, artillery, and transport assets that keep the assault supplied.

Off Operation

Sustained activity at another objective creates an opportunity report. Command may select it during a later LULL.

Foothold

A capture outside the active operation is not instantly integrated. Hold it and connect logistics so campaign benefits can follow.

Command Net's position report tells you whether you are in the main effort, a support area, a foothold, or operating independently.

03 / Shared Treasury

Every purchase is a theater decision

Players and the AI commander spend the same side treasury. Equipment, recruits, vehicles, construction, support, minefields, replacements, and forward depots all compete for available resources.

Balance

Total unspent side resources. Fresh EAST and WEST campaigns begin with 5,000 each.

Committed

Funds reserved separately for active operations. They remain in the balance but cannot be spent by unrelated purchases.

Available

Balance minus commitments. Store checkout and normal commander spending use this amount.

Income

Connected, integrated objectives generate resources every three minutes. Contested territory produces less.

A large purchase can remove the treasury headroom required to open or sustain a supporting offensive. Check available funds before committing the shared treasury.

04 / Logistics

Shared funds authorize; local supplies fulfill

Local Supplies are the stock currently available at one node. Supply Limit is the most that node can hold. A purchase or replacement needs both shared funds and enough supplies at the fulfilling node.

HQLimit 10,000 / +2,500 every 3 min

Root of the network. Supports every Store category and commander force package.

DepotLimit 6,000 / +800 every 3 min

Full forward sustainment and a commander replacement source.

FOBLimit 4,000 / +400 every 3 min

Gear, recruits, ground vehicles, armor, helicopters, statics, logistics, and commander replacements.

COPLimit 800 / +100 every 3 min

Light local sustainment for gear, recruits, and cars. It cannot source commander replacements.

Node States

CONNECTED works normally. STRAINED is under pressure or at 25% supplies or less. ISOLATED has no valid route. DISABLED has lost its anchor.

Supply Shipment

Costs 300 shared funds and 300 local supplies at its origin. Deliver it to a different friendly node to restore up to 1,500 supplies there.

05 / Bases and Forces

Build the support network you need

FOBs and COPs are persistent operational positions, not movable supply containers. Their location and route connection determine what players and command can generate there.

FOB

Each side's first successful FOB deployment is free. Later FOBs cost 1,500 shared funds. FOBs provide Store access, recruitable AI, heavier vehicles, shipments, and commander replacements.

COP

A cheaper light position for local gear, recruits, and cars. Use it to hold ground without creating a full replacement hub.

Store

The Store replaces the Arsenal and request menu. Catalog availability follows the active base type, shared funds, and local supplies.

Recruitable AI

Recruits join the purchasing player's group and consume both shared funds and local supplies.

A base outside connected integrated territory remains isolated. Capture and integrate the route, or build where the network already reaches.

06 / Command Intelligence

Act on what your side actually knows

Command Net combines commander intent, objective state, sustainment, player position, opportunities, footholds, and maintained intelligence. It is the primary campaign picture.

Friendly Logistics

Your side sees its complete node list, local supplies, supply limits, state, and active routes. Foreign nodes must never appear as friendly.

Enemy Logistics

Enemy HQs and supply nodes appear only when recovered intelligence reports them. Reports show captured information and expire.

Defender Warning

During enemy PREPARE, defenders may see only a coarse threatened sector until contact, combat, or recovered intelligence reveals the target.

Command Net

Open it with the configured Operations Panel CBA keybind. The default binding is Ctrl+Shift+O.

Start each session by reading commander's intent, checking available funds and local supplies, then choosing the main effort, sustainment, security, or opportunity role.
