Your role in the campaign

You fight alongside an AI commander. Choose where your squad can help; the commander coordinates the wider war.

1. Find a useful mission

Open Operations with Ctrl+Shift+O. An offensive progresses through gathering forces, assembly, reconnaissance, assault and holding captured territory. Select a mission to see its location and current progress. Defense and garrison work have a separate filter.

2. Capture and hold

Ground forces must reach and control an objective. Keep a holding force there while it integrates into friendly territory. Unsupported footholds need a secure connection to the rest of your side.

3. Prepare and support your squad

Use a connected base’s Store for equipment, recruits and vehicles. Purchases use shared funds and local supplies. Tactical Support provides artillery, strikes and air patrols when an appropriate asset, supplies and funds are available. Pick the package, designate a map target and inspect its readiness before requesting it.

4. Keep the front supplied

The Supply tab shows connected nodes and their stocks. Delivering supplies helps bases sustain purchases and replacements. Development lists construction projects and delivery opportunities. Combat and broken supply routes pause construction.

5. Understand the budget

Connected, integrated territory earns income. Replacements and essential logistics take priority over construction. The commander protects working capital and funding for the next replacement batch; emergency military needs can use available cash. Funds already committed to a transaction remain protected.

6. Check your save

The footer shows the last successful save in this session. The Funds tab shows the autosave interval and any failure. A failed save must be investigated in the server RPT. An admin can request a new save there.

Enemy strength is reported intelligence. “Unknown” means your side has no usable report; it does not mean the objective is empty.
