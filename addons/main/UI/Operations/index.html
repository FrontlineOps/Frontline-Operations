Frontline Operations Field Guide Campaign doctrine and sustainment

01 / Start Here

Your first five minutes

FLO is one persistent side campaign, not a chain of disconnected tasks. Your purchases, captures, deliveries, and combat decisions change the same theater picture used by every player and the AI commander.

1. Read Intent

Check the commander's intent, active operation, current phase, and phase timer before choosing where to go.

2. Locate Yourself

Read Your Position. It tells you whether you are in the main effort, a support area, a foothold, or outside command's current plan.

3. Check Sustainment

Review Available treasury and the nearest node's Local Supplies. Both are required for most purchases and support requests.

4. Choose a Role

Join the main effort, secure its logistics, defend a threatened area, or create a future opportunity away from the operation.

5. Finish the Fight

Capturing an objective starts the hold. Stay through SECURE so enemy pressure cannot immediately reverse the gain.

6. Recheck Command

Return to Command Net when the phase changes, a route fails, or the commander redirects the theater.

Best first move: follow the main effort and remain through SECURE. When that is already covered, protect its logistics or report pressure at another objective.

02 / Campaign Cycle

How the theater advances

Each operation moves through six phases. The phase tells you what command is trying to accomplish and whether the front should be resting, attacking, or holding a gain.

Lull

Command selects the next reachable effort while formations recover and logistics refill. This is the theater reserve period.

Prepare

The target, budget, doctrine, routes, and intelligence picture are set. Campaign groups do not move during PREPARE.

Assault

A doctrine-adjusted opening force commits as one mass. Follow-on waves replace losses or exploit progress at controlled intervals.

Secure

Friendly forces hold the target, defeat remaining resistance, and prevent the objective from reversing.

Consolidate

The captured objective is connected to command and logistics. Normal campaign benefits begin once integration succeeds.

Recovery

Orders and unused funds release, formations rebuild, and the operation closes before command returns to LULL.

The theater can run one main operation and up to two supporting operations. Operation width is driven by force readiness, logistics, treasury, distinct axes, and defensive pressure, never by connected player count.

03 / Operations

Main effort, support, and tempo

An operation is a funded campaign commitment with its own target, source, assigned formations, opening mass, follow-on limit, and phase deadline.

Main Effort

The primary axis receives the largest opening force, priority funding, and the clearest command intent. Only one operation owns this role.

Supporting Efforts

Up to two smaller operations may open on distinct targets and supply sources when the theater has enough capacity to sustain them.

Opening Mass

Command waits for the required package, then commits it atomically. It does not pretend one or two isolated squads are a full assault.

Approach Corridors

Groups receive compact parallel lanes so formations converge on the same battle instead of scattering across the objective.

Follow-On Waves

Later waves launch on the operation's cadence and replace losses or exploit momentum. They remain bounded by the operation package.

Shaping and Feints

Deception forces use movement orders toward another credible target. They do not count as the operation's committed ATTACK mass.

Allocation

Command reserves part of the shared treasury for each operation. Unused allocation returns when the operation ends or withdraws.

Support Area

The source objective, routes, depots, artillery, and transports behind an operation are part of the fight even when they are not on the target.

Activity outside active operations is not ignored. Sustained pressure can become a reported opportunity that command evaluates during a later LULL.

04 / Formations and Doctrine

Named units with persistent experience

Command groups compatible forces into named formations of three to six groups. Each formation keeps a lead group, branch, readiness, experience, rank, role, objective, and battle history across campaign saves.

Rank

Formations progress from GREEN to REGULAR, VETERAN, and ELITE. Surviving combat and successful operations build experience.

Readiness

Movement and combat drain readiness. Connected reserve and recovery rebuild it. Readiness changes both virtual combat power and the physical AI skill applied when units activate.

Breakthrough

Masses two additional opening groups and one additional follow-on group, then may exploit a linked weak objective after success.

Deception

Sends a temporary ASSAULT-only feint toward a separate credible target before the main opening. Survivors return when the feint expires.

Elastic Defense

Withdraws a pressured formation from a bounded, noncritical salient to preserve it. Capitals and critical objectives are never abandoned this way.

Counterattack

Adds one opening group when command responds to pressure and attempts to reverse enemy momentum.

Economy of Force

Uses the minimum package on a lower-priority axis: eight opening groups for the main effort, five for support, and follow-ons of no more than four.

Command Roster

Command Net lists your side's formations, rank, readiness, role, active strength, and current objective. Enemy formation doctrine remains classified.

Doctrine changes how forces are committed; commander spending posture changes how money is protected. They are separate systems.

05 / Player Role and Capture

Presence starts the capture; control finishes it

Players are the flexible force between command intent and battlefield reality. Your role depends on where you fight, what the active operation needs, and whether the area can be held after contact.

Main or Support Effort

Fight on command's selected axis, help create local superiority, and remain after the marker changes so SECURE can complete.

Logistics and Security

Protect source objectives, routes, bases, shipments, artillery, and transports. An isolated network can stop replacements and purchases.

Opportunity

Pressure away from an active operation can create a report for command. It is useful reconnaissance and shaping, not an instant new operation.

Sustained Dominance

Objective ownership requires maintained friendly control. A drive-by or momentary enemy absence is not enough to secure campaign ownership.

Active Target Capture

Capturing the operation target advances it into SECURE and CONSOLIDATE. Friendly presence must survive the counterpressure.

Unsupported Foothold

An off-operation capture becomes a foothold. Hold it and connect it to friendly command and logistics before expecting normal campaign benefits.

Leaving immediately after capture can reverse the gain. The Your Position panel always states your relationship to command's current effort.

06 / Shared Treasury

Every purchase is a theater decision

Players and the AI commander spend the same side treasury. Store purchases, recruits, vehicles, construction, tactical support, replacements, operations, and development all compete for the same money.

Balance

Total unspent side resources. Fresh EAST and WEST campaigns begin with 5,000 each.

Committed

Money reserved for active operations or funded projects. It remains in the balance but unrelated spending cannot use it.

Available

Balance minus commitments. Store checkout and normal commander spending use this amount.

Income

Connected and integrated objectives pay every three minutes. Contested pressure reduces their output.

Spending Posture

EMERGENCY, CONSERVE, BALANCED, and SURPLUS decide how aggressively command spends and how much reserve it protects.

Competing Priorities

Operation reservations protect offensives, but support, replacements, player purchases, shipments, and development still draw from what remains available.

A player's purchase has a real campaign cost, and the commander's spending affects what players can afford. Check Available before committing to a plan.

07 / Logistics

Shared funds authorize; local supplies fulfill

Local Supplies are the stock currently held at one node. Supply Limit is the maximum it can hold. Most purchases and replacements require both shared treasury and enough Local Supplies at the node fulfilling the request.

HQ10,000 limit / +2,500 per 3 min

The network root. It supports all Store categories and commander force packages.

Depot6,000 limit / +800 per 3 min

Full forward sustainment and a commander replacement source.

FOB4,000 limit / +400 per 3 min

Full player Store access and a commander replacement source.

COP800 limit / +100 per 3 min

Light gear, recruits, and cars. It cannot source commander replacements.

Node States

CONNECTED works normally. STRAINED is pressured or low on stock. ISOLATED has no valid friendly route. DISABLED has lost its anchor.

Routes and Integration

Ownership alone is not enough. Forward nodes need connected, secured territory before command can reliably source forces and supplies through them.

Supply Shipment

A shipment costs 300 treasury and 300 Local Supplies at its origin. Deliver it to another friendly node for up to 1,500 supplies.

Project Contribution

A shipment may instead be assigned to a funded Development project and delivered inside its objective area to accelerate construction.

When a purchase fails despite enough treasury, check the selected base's Local Supplies and route state. Available money cannot replace missing local stock.

08 / Regional Development

Invest in the territory behind the front

Command can fund persistent Revenue and Development projects at secure connected objectives. Projects reserve treasury first, then consume Local Supplies while construction is active.

Revenue

Increases that objective's campaign income. Repeated levels can turn ordinary territory into a major long-term source of treasury.

Development

Reduces project costs around the objective. Completed Development levels also expand how many projects the side can run at once.

Multiple Projects

Each side may construct several projects simultaneously while it remains within its current project capacity.

Funding and Construction

Command commits the full treasury quote before work begins. Active construction then draws Local Supplies from the objective's logistics network.

Player Contribution

Assigned Supply Shipments can provide up to 25 percent of a project's required supplies and record the contributing player.

Paused Work

Combat pressure or a broken logistics connection pauses construction without silently consuming progress.

Capture

Completed branches retain a 75 percent level floor after capture. Unfinished funding is released when the owning side can no longer continue it.

Development Net

This separate command view shows project levels, quotes, funding, construction progress, Local Supplies, capacity, and contribution status.

09 / Bases, Store, Recruits

Build useful positions, not movable containers

FOBs and COPs are persistent operational positions. Their placement and network connection determine what players can purchase and what command can source nearby.

First FOB

Each side's first successful FOB deployment is free. A failed placement does not consume that free deployment.

Later FOBs

Additional FOBs cost 1,500 shared treasury. They support gear, recruits, ground vehicles, armor, helicopters, statics, logistics, and commander replacements.

COP

A COP costs 600 and provides light gear, recruitable AI, and cars. It does not become a full commander replacement source.

Persistent Placement

Bases are deployed directly through their dedicated interface. They are not physical supply containers that must be dragged across the map.

Store

The Store replaces the Arsenal and request menu. Its catalog follows the active base type, shared treasury, and that base's Local Supplies.

Recruitable AI

Purchased recruits join the player's current group and consume both shared treasury and Local Supplies.

An isolated base cannot fulfill its full role. Secure a connected route and keep the node supplied before depending on it for heavy purchases or replacements.

10 / Tactical Support

Request effects from assigned command assets

Tactical Support Net replaces default Arma support actions. Select a package, inspect its assigned assets and readiness, designate a map position, then transmit the request.

ARTY

Requests a field artillery fire mission from an assigned battery. Range, ammunition, treasury, Local Supplies, and battery cooldown all matter.

CAS

Requests a helicopter or jet strike against the designated point. Combat aircraft enter from off-map and may be defeated or driven off by enemy air defense.

CAP

Requests a timed combat air patrol over an area. CAP protects the airspace rather than striking one ground target.

Assigned Assets

The left panel lists the package's command assets, source, availability, and readiness. A package with no ready asset cannot launch.

Target Designation

Choose free terrain on the embedded map. The panel shows grid and danger-close status before transmission.

Authorization

The side needs enough Available treasury, the correct connected supply source, required Local Supplies, and clear player, objective, and asset cooldowns.

No ready asset, no valid source, out of range, insufficient money or supplies, danger-close restrictions, or an active cooldown will deny the request without charging the side.

11 / Command Intelligence

Act on what your side actually knows

Command Net combines commander intent, operations, formations, objective state, sustainment, player position, opportunities, footholds, and maintained intelligence into the primary campaign picture.

Friendly Picture

Your side sees its own logistics network and formation roster, including node stock, routes, readiness, rank, roles, and current assignments.

Enemy Logistics Intel

Enemy HQs and supply nodes appear only through recovered intelligence. Reports reveal captured information temporarily and then expire.

Defender Warning

During enemy PREPARE, defenders receive a coarse threatened sector. Contact, combat, or intelligence must reveal the actual target.

Classified Doctrine

Your own side can inspect formation doctrine and status. Enemy shaping details and formation orders remain hidden until normal intelligence exposes activity.

Session Order

Read intent, locate yourself, check Available treasury and Local Supplies, choose a role, then use support and intelligence views as the situation demands.

When Plans Change

Reopen Command Net after phase transitions, captures, route failures, support denials, or new opportunity reports. The theater picture is persistent and dynamic.

You are ready. Enter the theater, support the current intent, and return here whenever the campaign state stops being clear.
