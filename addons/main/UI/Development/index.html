How development works

Revenue improvements increase income from connected, integrated territory. Development improvements reduce future construction costs and increase the side’s project capacity.

Construction starts after its treasury quote is funded. It then consumes local supplies over time. Combat or a broken supply connection pauses progress. Assigned player shipments can speed construction within the project’s contribution limit.

Project estimates assume continuous supply and security. The commander chooses projects; this panel lets you inspect progress and assign nearby shipments.
